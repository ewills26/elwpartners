AI range assistant · working demo

12,000+ products. 28 catalogues. Now it's one question.

Today your range lives across 28 separate page-flip catalogues, with allergen detail in the app or behind the portal login. But a chef at one of the 500+ schools, hospitals and care homes you supply doesn't think in catalogues — they think in questions. Ask one the way they would:

Ask the whole range

Tap a question — each one is answered across every catalogue at once, with allergen flags attached. In the live version this sits on your website, in the ordering portal, or inside telesales.

Demo uses a small illustrative dataset standing in for the full range — sample codes, pack sizes and allergen flags. The live version reads your real product files and allergen data, stays in step with them, and only ever answers from them.

The question box is the smallest example

The same approach — AI working over the systems you already run, not replacing them — is where cost comes out and revenue goes in at wholesale scale:

  • Order intake: customer emails and voicemail orders read, keyed and confirmed automatically — no re-typing into the ERP (this one's already built — see it working on Celtica's order pile →)
  • Telesales co-pilot: the gap in a customer's usual order spotted before the call ends, not after
  • Substitutions: a short-stocked line answered with the nearest in-stock alternative, instantly, instead of a blank on the invoice
  • Back office: invoice and PO matching, credit-application checks, delivery queries answered without a phone call
