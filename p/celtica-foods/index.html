Where this goes: costs down, revenue up

The two demos above are the front door. The same approach applies across the operation:

  • Order intake and confirmation with no retyping — fewer errors, fewer credits
  • Spec sheets, allergen data and audit prep drafted automatically, always current
  • Demand patterns spotted early — cut to what will sell, waste less of what won't
  • Invoice queries and credit-control chasing handled without tying up the office
  • A real web presence, so buyers who hear the Celtica name can actually find it
None of this replaces butchers.

It removes the admin wrapped around them — the part that costs money and wins none.
