About us

A family business, still run the same way

The journey began in 1971, founded by the brothers Glyn and Elfyn as a contractor focused on land drainage, and now known as Jones Brothers (Henllan) Limited.

In the early days the brothers concentrated on field drainage and the laying of pipelines in and around their West Wales base. Their commitment to new ideas and technologies has since seen the company diversify into all areas of the construction industry.

The business expanded steadily from its base in Henllan, Ceredigion, and by the 1980s had a substantial regional contracting business. Today, based at Cross Hands in Carmarthenshire, we cover the whole building lifecycle, from the deepest ground, bridges and highways through to new build, refurbishment, industrial and housing.

Jones Brothers is a true family business, with the reins handed over to Gareth and still insisting on a personal, hands on approach to every project, always available to customers to talk. Our core values help differentiate us from our competitors, and we pride ourselves on our strong family principles of trust, honesty and respect.

With the support of around 200 full time staff and supply chain, the business has grown into one of the most significant construction businesses in its field. Working across Wales and the West, our teams are fully committed to providing a highly professional and personalised service, tailored to each customer's needs.

Social value and sustainability

Delivering value for the Welsh pound

Every project we take on carries a Community Benefits and Targeted Recruitment Plan, with metrics and KPIs tracked and reported at project progress meetings. We aim to create one new training or employment opportunity for every £1m of turnover in a 52 week period.

Community

Residents, traders and site neighbours are consulted and kept informed from pre start to completion on every scheme.

Local labour

Apprenticeships, the Wales Shared Apprenticeship Scheme and recruitment targets agreed with our approved supply chain.

Environment

Construction Environmental Management Plans on every project, targeting 95% of construction waste diverted from landfill.

Biodiversity

Habitat creation through landscaping, swales and planting, bat and bird boxes on our buildings, and hedgehog friendly fencing on all projects.
